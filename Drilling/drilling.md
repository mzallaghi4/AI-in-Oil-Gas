### Drilling Optimization with Machine Learning

ML is transforming drilling optimization from a reactive, experience-based practice into a proactive, data-driven science. The main goal is to maximize drilling efficiency while minimizing costs, risks, and non-productive time (NPT).

ML is applied across several critical drilling sub-domains:
1. ROP Prediction and Optimization
2. Wellbore Trajectory Optimization
3. Drilling Fluid (Mud) Optimization
4. Bit Wear and Failure Prediction
5. Drilling Risk and Event Prediction


**Problem Statement**

Predict: ROP

ROP =f(WOB,RPM,Torque,FlowRate,MudDensity,Depth)

where:
- ROP = Rate of Penetration (ft/hr)
- WOB = Weight on Bit
- RPM = Rotary Speed


Dataset: drilling_data.csv
